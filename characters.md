## SEAN (protagonist) — "Number 9", "Sean of nothing"
- Appearance: man in his thirties, emaciated from starvation, curly hair, sharp nose, sunken eyes, bare feet caked with mud, ragged shirt. Right-shoulder scratch from the panther.
- Past (ch1): slave on a noble's convoy, brazenly tripped a fellow slave to distract a guard and steal the lockpin; cold, cunning, mocked by the slaves, exiled out of the huddle for the freezing night — which gave him the space to pick his shackles. Escaped into the Ramed forest.
- Past wound: a village/greenfield childhood; a wall of fire burned everything; children screaming "Sean, run!" That night "changed everything." Details of village, family, the fire [OPEN — backbone of the story, do not invent].
- Personality: ruthless survivor first; survival belongs to the most cunning. Blinks little. Feels pity for no one openly. His hardness cracks rarely (the sorrow flashed when remembering the fire).
- Now (ch4): host of the Crippled King's Soul Star. The Star settled chest-deep: warmth "like an ember", new steadiness/strength in his hands. Gift so far: hears the dead tongue as his mother-tongue.
- Voice: dry, raw, steady. Bargainer. Uses false smiles as armor. Keeps hunger hidden at the table.
- Core Psychological Drive: self-definition — to become a MAN WITH A NAME, no chain, no file, no ledger page over him. He is done being owned.
- Emotional Weakness (his own): the buried past — the girl whose name he "buried in the ashes," the green field, the fire; PTSD shuts his heart as armor, and the moment that door cracks (the locust, a kind hand) he bleeds. Varn already touched it with the page. Also: freedom itself is his leashless want — and can be used as bait.
- Arc battery (locked 2026-08-20, HYBRID): ruthless survivor → king-by-outlasting. He rises only through his own competence (reading rooms, betting his life, out-maneuvering), never by birthright or longing. Wins the crown in spite of himself; pays for it in people and conscience. He is the seed of the Rebellion — a king people follow because he refused to die, not because he was promised a throne.
- Theme-driven design (locked): he is evolving into KEEN OBSERVATION, EFFECTIVENESS, RUTHLESSNESS. Reader tracks his past — village, the fire, slavery, the "Lun" wound — to understand the cost of that evolution. Every cruelty he commits must be traceable to the life taken from him.
