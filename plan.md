## Author decisions (canon, locked)
- World: the portal opened into the SAME world, but ~100+ years in the FUTURE.
- The Rebellion in the title is seeded by **SEAN** himself (not the Crippled King). The King is a tool/mentor, not the heart.
- Sean's arc: **a ruthless survival** first, ENTIRELY earned. He becomes king NOT by birthright or desire but by OUTLASTING and OUT-MANEUVERING — competence that refuses to be ignored. He never wants the crown; he earns it the way he earns everything (survivor's arithmetic), and it costs him constantly. Every leadership moment is reluctant, cornered, or self-interested; the nobility of "destiny" is an ironic echo, never the fuel.
- Tone: **BALANCED** — dark & brutal scenes vs. warmth, found-family, and hope. Tone shifts by arc.
- **Locked themes (2026-08-20, author):** LOVE, SACRIFICE, CRUELTY shape the narrative. The reader dives into Sean's PAST to see a man form into KEEN OBSERVATION, EFFECTIVENESS, RUTHLESSNESS. These three traits are Sean's destination — and the reader must see the cost of each (love lost → cruelty learned → sacrifice demanded).
  - Love → where Sean's personhood once lived (the green field, the family, the fire). Every cruelty must be earned against that loss.
  - Sacrifice → the Keeper's 20-year wait, the King's bargain, rebels on the scaffold, and eventually what SEAN himself gives up.
  - Cruelty → the engine that bends him; shown, never romanticized; equalized by the cost it extracts from him.
  - Past-arc: Sean's history (village, fire, slavery, the "Lun" whisper) is the load-bearing wall under every transformation he undergoes. Budget the reveals across sagas — do not dump it.

## Narrative guardrails (recorded 2026-08-20, friend's v3 — locked as rules)
1. **No Deus Ex Machina through the King.** The Crippled King has 112 years of knowledge — using it to solve everything would gut Sean's agency. Rules: (a) his help is CRYPTIC, partial, often framed as a question; (b) he REFUSES or goes silent at going moments to test Sean; (c) his intel can be stale/wrong/two-edged (112 years is old data). Sean must earn wins with his own wits — the pin & the panther were HIS, not the King's. Reader must never wonder "why does Sean exist if the King does it."
2. **The white-haired woman must be NAMED (or given an official title) by Chapter 7.** Reader burden is real. (Done: Elara of House Vance.)
3. **Breath chapters after the ch1–5 sprint.** The next beats are a battle of wits + information-gathering, not combat. Physical action returns later. Pacing rule: tension alternates — never back-to-back full-throttle action arcs.
4. **Earned Emotion & Survivor's Romance (locked 2026-08-20, author):** The story is NOT 100% grimdark. Emotion, humanity, and subtle romance are explicitly ALLOWED and DESIRED — they provide the light that makes the dark fantasy work. Sean and Elara's relationship is evolving into a genuine bond (and potential romance), but it must be written as a connection between two hardened, traumatized survivors. When Sean shows he cares, it should not sound like a romance novel trope; it should feel like a man realizing he hasn't completely lost his heart. The King is also allowed to show genuine grief and love for the past, spoken with the weariness of an ancient monarch — not conversational, but ancient and sorrowful.

## Current volume: Saga 1 — "The Host"
Focus: Sean wakes into the future, negotiates survival against Vex/the woman, discovers what the Star means for his body, and decides whether this world is worth fighting for.
