## RELEASE-DAY PROTOCOL (once live)
1. Move one chapter from BUFFER to PUBLISHED.
2. If that chapter resolves or advances an open thread, cross/re-rate it in ledger.md.
3. Re-check: did today's hook still hold, or was it already spent by a later chapter? If spent, promote the correct next chapter.
4. Log daily entry at bottom (date, chapter#, hook rating 1–5, notes).
5. Buffer at or below 10 → STOP writing new sagas, refill to 20 before continuing the release curve.
