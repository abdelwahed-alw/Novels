# WRITER'S RULES — The Beginning of the Rebellion

> **NON-NEGOTIABLE.** Violations = rewrite. Read this before every chapter.

---

## CHARACTER INVARIANTS (NEVER BREAK)

### Sean
- He is a **pragmatic ruthless survivor.** He does NOT panic, forget, or lose what he fought for.
- He went to hell for the girl. He does **NOT** let go of her hand. He pulls her with him. She is beside him during every chase, every escape, every confrontation.
- He never acts on emotion without calculating first. Even grief is sharp, not soft.
- His sword is **never drawn first** — he is not a soldier. He fights only when cornered. When he kills, it is decisive and without flourish.
- He **never** offers mercy to someone who just tried to kill him. That is Elara's line, not his.

### Elara (Keeper of the Ring)
- She is **competent and dangerous.** She is NOT a damsel. She does NOT cry helplessly.
- She holds the ring with **iron.** She is the one who decides who lives or dies there.
- When shocked, she **acts** — draws her sword, counts the cost, holds the line. She does not freeze.
- Her softness is **earned and private** — a crack in the ice that appears only in rare, controlled moments (a breath, a glance, a silence). Never public tears. Never helplessness.
- She knows how to use her sword. She holds it correctly. She draws it cleanly.

### The Crippled King
- His help is **cryptic, partial, and gated.** He goes silent to test Sean. He will NOT rescue Sean from consequences.
- His voice: low, dry whisper "like ash shifting in a cold hearth." Often calls Sean "slave," "boy."
- He **cannot** control the Star's power. He can only warn, guide, and withhold.
- His grief is **ancient** — 112 years old. It surfaces as sudden silence, not as tears.

### Vex
- He is **obsessed** with his work, not with cruelty for its own sake.
- He believes the Star can be extracted again ("the procedure is proven").
- He is contemptuous of Sean as a "slave" and a "starving dog."

### The Blank-Faced Girl
- **STATUS UPDATE (ch30-31): the Engine's hold is BROKEN — Entry 0-1 paid in full by Sean's surrendered name.** She breathes, grips, speaks, and helps. Her first word after a century: "Sean" (she HOLDS his name now; only she and the King carry it).
- **REVEAL DISCIPLINE STILL BINDS:** her actual NAME remains unsaid in prose (only its meaning: "what grows back"). Her FACE is described only partially/indirectly (jawline, lashes, a mouth's corner) — full description banked for a planned future moment. Do not spend the full reveal casually.
- Pre-restoration rules (no agency, moves only when pulled) applied to all scenes BEFORE ch30-31 and remain the reference for flashbacks.
- Her century of filing left marks: expect rust, slowness, a body remembering how bodies work. Do not make her instantly whole or articulate.

## LORE INVARIANTS (NEVER BREAK)

### The Engine & Extractions
- The King died **112 years ago.**
- Vex has extracted the Star **9 times across the years.** Each failed host rotted within a year.
- The **9 glass cases** in the Ninth Door contain **Vex's recent failed extractions** (weeks/months ago), NOT the King's original followers from 100 years ago.
- The King **watched Vex's recent extractions** — he was sealed in the Ninth Door but aware. "I watched all nine happen" = Vex's 9 recent extractions, NOT events from 100 years ago.
- If a soul in the Ninth Door recognizes a living character, that soul must be a **recent extraction** (weeks/months ago) — someone the living character actually knew. NOT someone from 100 years ago.

### Timeline
- The past era (Sean's village, the fire, slavery) was **~100+ years before "now."**
- Elara is **young** (not 100+ years old). She cannot personally know anyone from the King's era.
- Any character who "knew" someone must be from the **same era** — recent past, not the King's time.
- **MIRA MATH (LOCKED — never violate):** Mira Vance was an adult at the planting 112 yrs ago, then FILED ALIVE IN TRUST by the house — unaging (same mechanic as the blank-faced girl). Bore Elara ~28 yrs ago (physically mid-20s under trust-hold). Died/released ~20 yrs ago (= Elara's grief window + origin of her 20-year promise). Elara is ~28 now. NEVER age Mira linearly across the gap; NEVER make Mira die at the burning; NEVER birth Elara from an aged-out Mira.

### The Star
- The Star settles chest-deep. It gives gifts (Sean's: hearing the dead tongue as mother-tongue).
- The Star **cannot be extracted** without killing the host. 9 failures prove it.
- The Court's true prize is not the Star itself: it wants what the King **remembers** — "one name, one map, one night."

### The Ninth Door
- An Engine room under the mountain where torn-out Stars are "filed." Sealed nine times.
- The 9 cases contain **Vex's recent failed extractions** — rotted bodies, still moving, screaming without sound.
- When a case shatters, the soul breaks free. It is hungry, rage-filled, and recognizes people from its recent life.

## NARRATIVE GUARDRAILS (NEVER BREAK)

1. **No Deus Ex Machina through the King.** The King is cryptic and gated. He does not rescue Sean.
2. **Name the woman by chapter 7.** ✓ DONE (Elara, ch7).
3. **Breath chapters between action sequences.** After a big action chapter, the next chapter must slow down.
4. **Earn the emotion.** Every emotional beat must be earned through character action, not author declaration.
5. **Short punchy paragraphs.** Third-person limited, past tense, Sean's POV.
6. **Sean's thoughts in italics.** King's voice also in italics. Clean division.
7. **No plot armor.** Sean survives by cunning, not by luck.
8. **No info-dumps.** Reveal through action and dialogue, not narration.
9. **Cliffhangers must rotate** — tension, reveal, decision, action, twist. Never repeat the same type back-to-back.
10. **The girl's name and face are the novel's spine.** DO NOT REVEAL until the planned moment.

## PREVENTION CHECKLIST (run before writing every chapter)

- [ ] Does Sean still hold the girl's hand? (If separated, there must be a logical, in-text reason.)
- [ ] Is Elara acting competently? (Not crying, not freezing, not needing rescue.)
- [ ] Are any new characters from the correct era? (Recent past, not 100 years ago.)
- [ ] Does the King's help feel cryptic and gated? (Not convenient, not rescuing.)
- [ ] Is the emotional beat earned? (Through action, not declaration.)
- [ ] Are short paragraphs maintained? (No walls of text.)
- [ ] Is the girl still filed? (No sudden agency, no speaking, no independent action.)
