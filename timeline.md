# TIMELINE — The Beginning of the Rebellion

> Numbered events in story order. "Now" = present era. Keep chronological, append as chapters add events.
> Chapters are the highest-resolution clock; timeline gives the long view.

## Locked dates
1. **Generation gap**: The Ramed-forest escape (Sean's past era) is ~100+ years before the "now" of ch4+.
2. **112 years before now** — the Crippled King died (killed; Night Court called him a plague).
3. **~20 years before now** — the woman's promise/sovereign patience that Sean's presence finally answers begins [OPEN]. Vex's extraction regime of the King's Star (9 extractions) spans this window.

## Story timeline (as written)
1. (ch1) Convoy day: Sean trips a slave, steals the lockpin, is shunned/exiled overnight; picks his shackles; escapes into the Ramed forest.
2. (ch1, night) Sean hides in a tree; the black panther finds him.
3. (ch2, same night) Sean blinds the panther's eye; both fall; panther nearly kills him at the tree-roots; the hunters' guns scare it off; the dogs are unleashed with an antidote; Sean flees toward a river.
4. (ch2) Sean finds the reddish-purple portal; bidden by instinct he steps back, then the dogs lunge — he stumbles through; portal vanishes.
5. (ch3) Sean wakes in the white laboratory, bound to a metal bed. Vex dismisses him as "another failure." The woman stops Vex from pulling the silver lever (the Engine out of power). She injects Sean with blue fluid; in the void he touches the white Soul Star; it merges with his soul; the King whispers "Take care of my body." Seconds passed outside.
6. (ch3–4, night) Sean wakes again; finds new strength; the Keeper and Vex examine him with the glass disc; the Star registers as "settled." Vex wants it extracted back; the Keeper refuses ("nine times... rotten"). Sean negotiates — food, freedom of movement — and is unlocked. He walks the ring, sees the Night Court below ("where they string up rebels"), memorizes the exits.
7. (ch4, night) In his barred room, the Crippled King speaks for the first time since ch3: teaches/warns, describes Vex's ledger. Vex — and "they" — were waiting for "someone the Engine recognized." Sean lies awake all night.
8. (ch5, next dawn) Sean bluffs his way clear of Vex's needle (the King coaches him, counts footsteps; the Star's ember "turns" and shakes Vex). The woman ("the Keeper") takes Sean to the ring's high windows: the scaffold where the Court hangs "rebels" by threes. One prisoner curses a name — "Lun" — that makes the King go still for the first time. A man in the crowd grips Sean's arm and says the Warden has been "expecting you for a hundred years." The King goes silent.
9. (ch6, same day) Sean confronts the Grey Hand's agent (a "Brother Silence") at the scaffold; outmaneuvers the knife-grab; the Keeper overrules the Hand — the Star is "sealed" until the Engine recharges. In the Engine room the King reveals the first sliver: "Lun was a woman." Sean's buried memory almost breaks loose (the girl in the fire whose name he "buried with the ashes"). The Grey Hand was not retreating — they're ENCLOSING THE RING. Sean realizes the move is against the KEEPER, not him.
10. (ch7, that night) The siege of the ring — settled by WITS, not blades. Sean reads the siege as a negotiation (the Warden needs a WILLING host — extraction never preserves memory); confronts Elara of House Vance; she reveals House Vance burned first with the King, and the Warden moves to snuff the house's last candle. Sean's gambit: he walks into the Night Court ALONE, on his own terms. Ring unseals; Vex's projects end. The King's gated warning: "The woman is not what you think she is... Watch her hands." Elara presses her brother's ring, hides her shaking hand.
11. (ch8, night, the Court) Sean meets CASSIAN VARN face to face in his warm, guardless study. Varn keeps pages from before the fire — shows Sean a glassed drawing of a child's hand holding a locust; the Court has filed Sean's small songs "longer than he has been alive." The Engine is dying; Varn reveals the map = the last chart of the old power-grid's birthplace-and-burial, which dies with the Engine. THIS is why today / a hundred years. Varn's offer: "Map the house for me. One room. One night. And I will give you the only thing a man like you has never been offered: you." (Freedom — a name, a life.) The King speaks Sean's name then falls silent for the first time in eight chapters.
12. (ch9, the next days — the Court's paper city) Sean is given a clerk's room (test: knife left untouched, bread taken); he maps the Court's routines (patrol beats, meal cart, clerks' circuit) and learns "bread days" = the Warden moves a big file. He meets NELLA — a young archive healer, open, naive, kind. She treats his panther-wound; reveals the NINTH DOOR: an Engine room under the mountain where torn-out Stars are "filed," sealed nine times — "one more name and none of us leave." The King's gated warning: Nella is a "dove in a slaughterhouse," already filed, and will be used as a lever to tame Sean. Sean denies caring; his hand betrays him. New rope threaded in the scaffold yard below.
13. (ch10, third evening) The dinner of doors. Varn reveals the BLANK PAGE: no Sean entry exists in any surviving ledger — the Engine cannot see Sean; "the only man in a thousand years it ever brought home that it never once sent for." (Pages exist for the locust, the grass, even the girl — but not the boy.) The map's terms negotiated: Sean walks the map's one place for one night; NELLA goes to the ring under the Keeper's seal by morning; the ring freed for a full year; the Ninth Door opened while Sean stands in the place; freedom filed under Sean's name. Sean's knife-outburst over Nella gives Varn the "page" he wanted — proof of Sean's heart. Deal sealed with a handshake.
14. (ch11, that same night — the walk) Sean, Varn, Brother Silence walk to the first-light bowl — a ring of black stones around an iron plate. Sean stands; the King's memory opens: the old city alive, the King before the fire, Elara's mother beside him choosing to burn. Then — the GIRL appears at the clearing's edge, face BLANK, erased by the Engine, not lost but filed. Sean reaches for her; fails. The vision shatters. The Ninth Door cracks open in the city's bowels. A child's voice calls "Run" through the dark — the same word from Sean's fire. The King whispers: "She is calling you, Sean. She has been calling you since the night you forgot her name."
15. (ch12, after the walk) The return to the Court. The Ninth Door is open and the paper city knows — guards tense, clerks whispering. Varn seals the lower corridors; warns Sean: "decide what you are willing to lose." Sean goes to the ring — Nella is delivered (Varn's collateral, not a gift). Sean tells Elara about the vision: her mother chose the King, held his hand. Elara breaks — "She never held anyone's hand. Not even mine." The King speaks without gate for the first time, telling Elara the truth: her mother loved him, not the cause. Elara cries for the first time in twenty years. The King warns: the Door holds mistakes, not just gentle things. Sean decides: he will go back — not for Varn, not for the map, but for the girl's name.
16. (ch13, morning after) MASTER CORVYN confronts Sean on the south stair — "slave," "mistake," the arrogant face-slap. Sean neutralizes him with records (bread-day signal, "liaison did not read"). Corvyn delivers the envoy message: the NIGHT COURT has filed a formal claim on Sean. Varn's deal doesn't hold against a formal claim. Sean has until the next bread day. Elara reveals the envoy arrived this morning. Sean decides: he goes back to the Ninth Door TONIGHT, before the bread day. The King agrees: "Then we go tonight."
17. (ch14, that night) THE DESCENT. Sean tells Elara he's going — transactional, cold. She asks him to come back; he walks away. He navigates the sealed lower corridors using ch9 observations (patrol patterns, blind spots, the clerks' circuit gap). The chain-lock counts nine scratches and opens. Inside: a vast dark room, iron shelves, NINE GLASS CASES holding rotted bodies in luminous fluid — the failed extractions, still moving, still screaming without sound. They turn toward Sean. The King confirms: "They are the ones who carried me and failed. They hate you because you carry what they could not keep." Sean steps forward.
18. (ch15, inside the Ninth Door) Sean walks the nine cases — numbered 1 through 9. The ninth is cracked, the fluid dark, the body more decayed: "the closest, almost lived, torn apart at the threshold." King's guilt: "I watched all nine happen. I could not stop it." Sean reaches the back wall — the GIRL stands in a niche, face blank, reaching not with hands but with something in the space between. He remembers her REAL NAME (not the fire-name, not the Court's file — the name she whispered in the green grass). He touches her hand. The ninth soul SCREAMS — real sound, not silence. The case cracks. The body pulls free. All nine cases shake. The chamber cracks. The King: "You've opened the door that was sealed for a reason. They're awake. They're hungry." Sean does not let go of her hand.
19. (ch16, escape) REWRITTEN. The NINTH SOUL — MAREK — breaks free. Sean grabs the GIRL from the niche and DOES NOT let go of her hand. She runs beside him the entire escape through the lower corridors. Sean navigates using ch9 observations. Emerges in the ring. Elara sees what follows — draws her sword cleanly, holds the corridor. The ninth soul stops, says "King," then recognizes Elara: "Marek" — one of Vex's RECENT failed extractions (weeks/months ago), a guard/brother-in-arms Elara knew from the ring. NOT someone from 100 years ago. Grief turns to rage. Elara holds the line while Sean and the girl flee to the top of the ring. King's final line: "That is what the Engine does." Sean: "The price is paid by the man who carries you. Not by the man who follows." The ninth soul is loose in the ring. The girl is with Sean, still filed, still blank-faced.
20. (ch17, dawn) REWRITTEN. Vex (male) arrives on the roof: the envoy found the ninth case shattered, all eight remaining cracking. A JUSTICE (old woman, black cloak) waits in the war-hall. Sean offers to recapture the ninth soul in exchange for the girl. Justice refuses — Court will not give the girl. Choice: recapture the ninth soul and seal the Door, or the Court seals the ring and everyone in it. Elara confronts the Justice on protocol — "You're in my ring. You owe me a courtesy." Justice forced to "ask" instead of "command." Sean accepts. Demands the girl come with him. Elara offers to go; Sean refuses — "You stay here. You hold the ring." She resists. He insists. He goes back down into the Door with the girl. The ninth soul is still loose in the ring's lower floors. Buffer: 17/0.
21. (ch18, dawn) REWRITTEN. Sean descends into the Ninth Door with the girl (holding her hand, still filed). The ninth case is SHATTERED and EMPTY — Marek is gone (loose in the ring). The other eight cases are cracked, bodies stirring, waking. Vex is already down there, trying to recapture the 8 loose souls with syringes. Sean sees Vex's obsession in action. Sean makes a pragmatic deal: he leaves the 8 souls to Vex; Vex owes Sean (when I call, you answer; when I need, you provide; when Court asks, you say nothing) and lets Sean/the girl walk away. Sean returns to Elara at the top of the stairs. Confirms the deal. Buffer: 18/0.
22. (ch19, that morning) THE ALMOST-KING. Nella unaccounted for since the night breach. Real scream from the infirmary stores — Marek cornered her but did NOT hurt her; he stood between her and the door, fixated on the Star. His motive revealed: not hunger — LONGING. "I almost carried it." The King apologizes to him through Sean ("Almost is the cruellest word the Engine knows"). Sean turns the ember (budgeted gift paid off) — Marek kneels and reaches into the light even though it burns him. Elara kills Marek cleanly: "He was mine... This one is not yours to carry" (to Nella). Body wrapped, burned with rites under his true name: MAREK OF THE WATCH. END HOOK: bread-day bells ring across the city — the Warden is moving a file. Something walks toward the ring with a page in its hands.
23. (ch20, bread day, noon) THE PAGE. Varn arrives on bread day — no guards but Brother Silence, Corvyn trailing. Registers Marek's death as "died holding his post" (record-scrubbing). Delivers SEAN'S PAGE: freshly inked, no longer blank — "SEAN. OF NO FIXED ORIGIN. FILED UNDER: SENT FOR." The Engine can SEE Sean now; it is writing everything he is (scars, field, fire, HER). When the page fills, the ENGINE collects what it catalogued. Only way to close the page: walk the map — the place, the night. Deadline: TONIGHT ("the Engine is writing fast"). King goes afraid again at Varn. Corvyn lingers with a kept secret. The page keeps writing itself on the bench.
24. (ch21, that night) THE SECOND WALK. Elara runs prep like a siege in reverse; warns: "The price is always revealed after the handshake... We do not fear fire. We fear unfinished things." Festival-dark climb to the bowl. Terms stated: stand in the place until the page closes. Memory opens DEEPER this time — the last night of the old world: REVEAL — the King chose his grave. He told ELARA'S MOTHER (MIRA Vance) to bury him on ground the Engine remembers: "If I die scattered I am nothing. If I die HERE — planted — then I am a seed." "They will call it plague. Plague is what the living call a harvest they didn't plan." Origin of "one name, one map, one night": he charged Mira to keep all three until someone comes who can read them. Mira, who never held anyone, held his hand and promised. The Engine begins COLLECTING early — gold script peeling off Sean's skin toward the glowing plate. The King counters: writes HIS OWN ending on Sean's page in ember-characters — "THE GRAVE IS MINE... THE ACCOUNT CLOSED BY ITS OWNER" + a burned-white signature-name. All collected script falls back INTO Sean. Page signed and shut — EXCEPT ONE LINE: cross-reference to HER still-open file. Varn delivers the true price: her file must close first, "and files do not close while anyone is still holding on to what they contain." END HOOK: Sean looks at their joined hands.
25. (ch22, same night → dawn) THE OPEN LINE. Sean refuses to let go: "Then the line stays open... She held mine when it mattered. I'm returning the courtesy." Accepts half-visibility (open line = a straw the Engine can drink through forever). Varn sits on the bowl rim — tells the parable of the woman who drowned her child's name: erasure is NOT resolution, it is postponement; the ledger stayed open 20 years because the debt was never paid. MECHANIC LOCKED: "Debts erase faces. Payments restore them." The girl's face returns when the account is settled. The original entry (the default, the default-er, the sum) lies somewhere in the 400,000-page archive. Varn departs; Corvyn keeps his question behind his teeth. KING'S COST MANIFEST: ember goes QUIET for the first time since the merge — not gated but absent-feeling; voice thin, far; "tired in a new way." Dawn return: Elara asks what it cost — "A line. And something I don't have words for yet." Sean's plan, final line: "Now we rob the Warden." [END OF ARC 1]
26. (ch23, six days later) THE DEEP STACK — ARC 2 OPENS. Ring scrubbed of smoke; city back to hunger; girl unchanged; ember still quiet six days — Sean talks to the silence. War council: target = the ORIGINAL ENTRY (default, default-er, sum); problem = pre-fire erasure entries live in the DEEP STACK — the old cellar archive flooded and sealed after "the burns," 400k pages, uncatalogued by any living soul. Team locked: Nella leads, Sean carries, Elara holds the door. King's voice returns ONCE after six days, thin and far: "I am still here. Do not dig for me. Dig for the page." HOOK: Corvyn arrives alone at the gate under parley-blow — full dress uniform, empty hands — wearing a clerk's satchel bulging with documents bound by VARN'S OWN UNBROKEN BLACK WAX SEAL. His pitch: "I've brought you the thing you're going to rob the Warden to find! No point being robbed for it when I'm prepared to be paid."
27. (ch23–24, same day) PARLEY. Elara admits Corvyn under Keeper's law (+ her own clause: no lies spoken directly). Corvyn's account: three days ago summoned to Varn's study — his name appeared on the day-sheet in still-drying ink; on the desk lay the sealed satchel with a one-line instruction: "Deliver to the Keeper's ring. Unopened. By bread-day-plus-six." When he returned to ask, the study was cold, the sheet blank, clerks swearing the Warden hadn't left his chambers in two days. Corvyn's PRICE: not gold — survival and standing; when the ledger settles he walks out alive, employed or gone; he wants SEAN to owe him. Nella reads the seal (grandmother's medicine-wax lore): wax-over-wax, the underlayer takes the reader's heat and remembers every hand. Under-wax address discovered: "TO THE HAND THAT HOLDS HERS." Sean opens it — the wax splits politely for him. Contents: decades of copy-paper topped by ENTRY 0-1: "DEBTS UNPAID, CARRIED FORWARD FROM BEFORE THE FIRE. DEBTOR OF RECORD: —" and below the header the page has been TORN — one line wide, clean, deliberate, long ago. The debtor's name was removed before this copy's ink dried.
28. (ch25, dawn after parley) BALANCE UNCHANGED. Sorting the satchel on the war-hall floor: NOT ballast — 31 ERASURE-ENTRY COPIES spanning decades, every debtor line torn IDENTICALLY (clean pull, low angle, left-to-right, thumbnail-deep) — one censor technique across a century; tears match the girl's own file. The girl is an ENTRY TYPE, not a mistake. Sean asks the King WHICH fire ("carried forward from before the fire"); the ember answers once, close, with a riddle and withdraws: "How many fires does your one small life have, slave? Count them. Then ask which ones I stood in." Nella finds the false-bottom sheet — FRESH clerk-stock, today's ink, A RECEIPT stamped with a circle-bisected-by-a-line (Warden's ring-sign pressed INTO page, not from wax): "RECEIVED OF THE HAND THAT HOLDS HERS: one night standing in the place; one ending, royal, spent willingly. BALANCE UNCHANGED." Corvyn IDs the mark as sealing Varn's PRIVATE VAULT — meaning the receipt issued from wherever the ring-sign is a key. LOCATION LOCKED: the vault lies beyond the deep stack; the flooded levels are storage, not damage. The heist now has a delivery address.
29. (ch26, dusk two days later) THE CHUTE — INFILTRATION BEGINS. Disguise: grief. Three healer-grey cloaks, one bier; THE GIRL RIDES THE BIER (filed = no breath, unreadable by clerks' compasses — "the safest of us inside"). Elara STAYS at the ring: "Hold the door means hold the other end"; her only order: "Bring them both back." Corvyn's last gift under guest-seal: forged mourner permits. Postern admitted at dusk; infirmary took the bier at second bell unsearched. Chute-room: sloping trough breathing cold; Nella's grandmother's last gift — waxed surgeon's bandage wound around an iron ring in the chute mouth as a descent line ("She didn't just learn turns. She learned out."). Trio descends hand-over-hand; bier lowered after. Flooded levels: ankle-deep water, black shelves 40ft+, coffin-sized oilcloth bundles, numbering systems older than the current court. NEAREST STACK — fresh labels, six days old: "RECEIVED OF THE HAND THAT HOLDS HERS: one night standing in the place / one ending royal spent willingly" — THEY ARE STANDING INSIDE THE DELIVERY VAULT; the King's ending shelved within arm's reach. NEXT BUNDLE OVER — ancient, label rewritten twice by dead hands, same formula: "RECEIVED FROM THE HAND THAT HELD HIS: ONE (1) KING. FILED ALIVE. PLANTED PER AGREEMENT."
30. (ch27, same night) FILED ALIVE. They leave the ancient bundle untouched ("We came for a debt, not for kings"). Nella leads deeper — grandmother's turns are a SPIRAL, rings aging inward toward zero-stack. As the bier passes, loose pages stir like grass acknowledging something walking through (the file recognizing its subject). Center: dry round chamber, one stone pedestal, TWO items only — grey Warden-ledger + the oilcloth bundle already home there. ENTRY 0-1 READ IN FULL, hand grown-up-out-of-the-page: "DEBTOR OF RECORD: MIRA VANCE. BORROWED: ONE PLANTING. ONE PLACE MADE TO REMEMBER. COLLATERAL HELD IN TRUST UNTIL PAID: ONE (1) FACE. ONE (1) NAME." — Mira bought the King's burial-ground with the girl's face and name; the collateral is HELD IN TRUST, NOT destroyed — her face exists somewhere on these shelves. King returns CLOSE, devastated: "She held my hand while she owed them everything... I never knew." — pays his own line-form: "some graves hold... because opening them shows what they're lined with." THEN: lamp-flames lean; footsteps without splash; VARN walks out of the flooded dark — coat dry, rooms arranging themselves around him. Reads the scene: "You are reading her mail, and she has waited a very long time to be read." Looks at the bier with a sum-finally-balancing expression: "She arrived. Good. We can finally discuss payment."
31. (ch28, same night) THE TERMS. Below water level nothing exists — parley agreed. Varn reveals SURETY MECHANIC: Entry 0-1 = TWO obligations — collateral (face + name) AND surety (living pledge of debtor's flesh): MIRA HERSELF, filed alive, unaging ~84 yrs; trust released her just long enough to bear ONE daughter (~28 yrs ago), then "took the years back with interest"; term ended 20 yrs ago (= Elara's grief window; Elara knows none of this). WRONG ACCOUNT EXPLAINED from the ledger: bowl-night + the King's ending were both credited toward the CLOSED surety account — "received the way the sea receives rain"; collateral untouched. Varn could have said so at the bowl; down here the telling costs him nothing. HIS MOTIVE: a file cannot pay its own debt — the hand holding collateral cannot settle its own claim; the house can NEVER close its own accounts; Sean = first outsider able to settle in a century. "I built no roads. I stopped sweeping one clear." He OFFERS the location: nine rows out, third ring, behind fire-damaged stacks — face wrapped, labelled HELD. THE TRAP NAMED: carrying collateral out without payment is not theft but DEFAULT — Entry 0-1 transfers WHOLE to the bearer; 31 torn names become 32. NAMES ARE CARRIED: the girl's name-collateral = pressure-grooved vellum, pen gone through, unreadable by eye, carried by Wardens of Varn's line three generations; speaks only when pressed into something softer. FINAL CHOICE FRAMED: which crime tonight — carry the face (default/transfer), or learn whose name Mira went to her grave unable to say.
32. (ch29, same night) WHAT GROWS BACK. Sean refuses the either/or frame — cheap crime first: presses the grooved leaf into warm lamp-tallow. The shape speaks in the DEAD TONGUE — only Sean can read it (translation gift paid again). Her name's MEANING revealed: "WHAT GROWS BACK" — the thing a field does the spring after the fire; a harvest-word; actual name still unrevealed to reader (spine intact). NELLA'S DETONATION: the formal old-tongue form of the name = the FOURTH VERSE of the forbidden scaffold song — "The Engine feeds and the city starves, but what grows back, grows back." It was never a rebellion rhyme; it's a LULLABY built on her NAME. The whole city has been singing her for a hundred years without knowing. They carry the bier nine rows out, third ring, behind fire-scarred stacks — the bundle wrapped in amber oilcloth, labelled HELD in the censor's neat murderous hand. THE KING OPPOSES: "Put it down, boy... Some prices are load-bearing... I did not spend my ending to watch you become the debt." First time he warns AGAINST an action to protect Sean. CLIFFHANGER: Sean's hand on the oilcloth, girl against his collarbone, Varn setting down the lantern "preparing to witness either a theft or a sacrament."
33. (ch30, same night) EQUAL WEIGHT. Sean's hand comes AWAY from the wrap — he asks instead: what does the house ACCEPT? Answer: NAMES, surrendered freely, spoken aloud by the owner's living mouth into the ledger's hearing — the one coin that ever moved name-collateral; faces can't pay faces; endings aren't legal tender. EQUAL WEIGHT RULE (Nella, grandmother's third law): a ransom weighs what it ransoms — her old-tongue load-bearing name needs equal weight, and only ONE name qualifies: SEAN'S, two days old, page-written, already carrying her weight via cross-reference. KING PLEADS AT FULL VOICE then breaks himself: "Ask me if I wouldn't do the same. Ask me, and then refuse to learn the answer, and put that bundle down forever." VARN'S COST WARNING: spent names are not freed but FILED — Sean would exist wrapped, labelled, held — and know it. "There is a reason my line carried her name instead of spending it." Sean's answer: "I've been property before. I know the shape of the walls." Nella hands him the tallow name wordlessly. Sean stands at the pedestal, ember silent at last — nothing left in 112 years of ash to say — and speaks. FIRST SYLLABLE OUT: all water in the flooded dark goes still; the first bell of dawn rings above; nine rows back the amber label changes from HELD to SPOKEN. Transaction mid-flight into ch31.
34. (ch31, dawn) PAID IN FULL. The name costs "like a nail costs the wood it leaves" — spoken on one knee. LEDGER WRITES: "PAID IN FULL / COLLATERAL: RETURNED TO SUBJECT / SURETY: DISCHARGED POSTHUMOUSLY / ACCOUNT CLOSED BY PAYMENT FROM OUTSIDE THE LEDGER / CROSS-REFERENCE: VOID / RECEIVED: ONE NAME. FILED." The vault exhales; label RELEASED. Varn seals the grey ledger with his own ring — "Four Wardens... My grandfather tore the copies. My father carried the name. I swept the road clear. It took a slave with no page." KING MECHANIC REVEALED: a spent name is not destroyed but HELD by whoever the ledger owes — THE GIRL NOW HOLDS SEAN'S NAME; only two souls in existence carry it: the King (who knew it before anyone spoke it) and her. THEN: THE GIRL BREATHES — first breath in a century; fingers GRIP his (first chosen act — the Engine's hold broken by payment); face surfacing like ink through parchment, partial, arriving (reader denied full view — spine intact); her first word, rusted, a century of silence in one wheel of sound: "…Sean." — the only person alive who can still call him that. Varn exits to be found nowhere; parting gift: "Ledgers cannot chase what they cannot name. You have become the second-blankest page in this house's history." Exit via chute as mourning detail. FINAL KING LINE, warm and terrible: "Now you are one of us, slave. Now you are ours."
35. (ch32, that morning) BLANK — THE RETURN. City oblivious; ring guards look STRAIGHT THROUGH Sean (second-blanket demonstrated live). The girl stops dead in her first sunlight in a hundred years, face up, eyes shut, drinking it. Sean tells her ALL of it flat — Entry 0-1, Mira's bargain, 84 years filed alive, the daughter born late, discharge 20 years ago. Elara's iron grief at the window: the Court reported fever; no body; family signed for effects — "a ring. A comb. Forty years of service and they gave her a comb." Her question to the King through Sean: "Do I thank him? Do I curse him? My house burned twice — once in fire, once in ledgers — both times for that man." KING'S ANSWER: "Both times it bloomed, too... Household book updated: healer, guest under seal, "a foundling with no entry anywhere in creation." Elara's quill hovers over NAME. 36. (ch33, same day, morning) HOW TO BE NO ONE. Infirmary: girl relearns eating (spoon→broth→egg triumph).
